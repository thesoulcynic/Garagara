## Quick scan: Ikazia Hospital Rotterdam

### What type of business is Ikazia Hospital Rotterdam?
Ikazia is a **general hospital (healthcare provider)** in Rotterdam, Netherlands.  
It operates in the **hospital and medical services** sector, delivering specialist clinical care.

### What do they do?
Ikazia provides **patient care and medical treatment**, including:
- outpatient consultations
- diagnostics (such as lab and imaging services)
- inpatient hospital care
- surgery and specialist treatments
- emergency and urgent care support
- maternity and other core hospital services
